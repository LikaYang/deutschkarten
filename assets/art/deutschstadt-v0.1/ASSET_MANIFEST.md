## Notes

- These assets establish `Deutschstadt Art Style V0.1`.
- NPC/building/drink assets should stay in the same warm orange/cream, realistic German everyday-life style.
- Do not use this style to imitate any existing game, animation, artist, or brand.
- Future A1/A2 card art should prioritize 10-20 concrete core words per unit.
- Future B1 card art should shift toward concept sketches and visual metaphors for abstract vocabulary.
